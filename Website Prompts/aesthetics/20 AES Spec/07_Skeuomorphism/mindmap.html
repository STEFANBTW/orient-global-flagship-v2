07 Skeuomorphism — Interactive Mindmap

  • 07 — Skeuomorphism
    • Ranked Industries
      • 1. Music Production Software / DAWs
        • Virtual mixing boards, equalizers, and studio hardware references feel physically satisfying and professionally exact.
      • 2. Gaming (Simulation Games)
        • Games simulating real-world environments use skeuomorphic UI to reinforce the simulation and deepen player immersion.
      • 3. Luxury Goods E-Commerce
        • Leather-textured product pages reinforce material quality and transfer premium tactile memory to the visitor.
      • 4. Financial Services (Heritage Institutions)
        • Wood-grain and leather textures signal tradition, permanence, and established institutional trust.
      • 5. Education Platforms (Children's)
        • Books that look like real books, notebooks that look like real notebooks — familiar physical metaphors dramatically reduce learning curves.
      • 6. Note-Taking / Personal Organization Apps
        • The original context for skeuomorphism's renaissance: Apple's Notes app, leather-bound calendar apps — users immediately understand the metaphor.
      • 7. Craft / Artisanal Brands
        • A coffee roaster whose website feels like parchment, a whiskey brand whose interface feels like aged wood — the material IS the brand story.
      • 8. Legal / Professional Services (Heritage)
        • Communicating institutional longevity through material references to libraries, leather, and formal paper documents.
      • 9. Cooking / Recipe Platforms
        • Recipe cards that look like index cards, cookbooks that look like real books — matching the physical experience of cooking.
      • 10. Subscription Boxes / Gift Products
        • The unboxing experience translated into digital — textured, material-rich interfaces that reference the physical gift experience.
    • Psychological Foundation
      • Familiarity Transfer
        • Borrows established meaning and emotional associations of physical objects and transfers them instantly to digital interfaces.
        • Most powerful when audience is non-technical OR when brand value is rooted in physical tradition.
      • Material Memory
        • Leather texture triggers remembered weight, smell, and warmth. Wood texture triggers remembered grain and solidity.
        • Creates emotional connections that flat design simply cannot access.
    • Color System
      • Palette Logic
        • Colors derived directly from physical materials — dictated by the material world, not brand color theory.

      • Sizing & Scale Rules
        • Body: 14px–16px (matches physical book comfort). Headlines: sized realistically for the object. NO 96px headlines — no physical notebook has one.
      • 📖 Open Typography Studio ↓
    • Spacing & Layout
      • Physical Reference Rule
        • Before any spacing decision: "What would this be in the real physical object?" — The physical reference IS the specification.
      • Material Spacing Specs
        • Notebook lines: 28px–32px. Legal pad margin rule: ~80px from left. Leather border: 20px–30px. Book gutter: inner margin wider than outer.
    • Borders, Shapes & Forms
      • Material Edges
        • No 1px solid borders — use leather stitching edges, page corner curls, machined metal bezels, or wooden edge-banding instead.
      • Bevel Effect
        • Top-left: rgba(255,255,255,0.3)→transparent gradient. Bottom-right: rgba(0,0,0,0.2)→transparent. Together = raised beveled surface.
      • Border Radius by Material
        • Buttons: 6px–10px. Notebook pages: 0px (square corners). Leather portfolio: 4px–8px. Metal panels: 2px–4px (machined precision).
    • UI Components
      • Navigation
        • Folder tabs protruding from content top. Book spine sidebar with different texture. Physical toggle/switch navigation buttons.
      • Form Elements
        • Inputs: paper recesses or metal frames with inner shadow. Checkboxes: physical form boxes with SVG check marks. Radios: circular physical selectors.

    • Animation & Motion
      • Button press: translateY(1px) in 80ms. Gradient inverts simultaneously. Release: 120ms ease-out.
      • Page turn: rotateY(0°→-180°) with perspective on parent. Duration 400ms–600ms.
      • Toggle: translateX slides thumb across track. background-position shifts to simulate physical rotation. 200ms.
      • Physics-based easing only — slight overshoot, natural deceleration. No abstract digital easing.
    • What to Strictly Avoid
      • ❌ Mixing material references on the same surface — leather + glass + paper creates material incoherence. One material per structural element.
      • ❌ Low-quality textures — pixelated or obviously tiled textures immediately break the illusion.
      • ❌ Physical impossibilities — a page that is also a tab that is also a folder cannot exist in physical reality.
      • ❌ Flat color buttons on textured surfaces — everything must be rendered. A flat button on leather looks unfinished.
      • ❌ Modern UI conventions breaking the metaphor — tooltips, modals, badges must all render in the material language.
      • ❌ Animating non-physical behaviors — a skeuomorphic page cannot fly across screen like a Material Design card.

Design Guidelines
Skeuomorphism Rules:
• Max headline: ~36px (physical scale)
• Body: 14px–16px for paper, 12px–14px for instrument panels
• Max weight: 700 (nothing feels punched through physical surface)
• Italic headlines preferred for luxury/leather contexts
