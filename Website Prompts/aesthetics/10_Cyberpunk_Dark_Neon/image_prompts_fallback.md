# Image Generation Prompts for Cyberpunk Dark Neon

If you run out of image generation quota, use these 10 highly-tailored Midjourney/DALL-E prompts to generate the photography assets for this aesthetic.

---

### 1. Person Portrait (photography_person_portrait.png)
**Prompt:** Cyberpunk portrait photography, subject wearing tech-wear, illuminated by harsh neon pink and cyan lights. Deep pure black shadows, gritty urban atmosphere, rain-slicked skin, high contrast, dark futuristic aesthetic.

### 2. Person in Environment (photography_person_environment.png)
**Prompt:** Cyberpunk environmental photography, a silhouette of a person standing in a massive, dark futuristic alleyway. Lit only by glowing neon signage reflecting off rain-slicked concrete, oppressive atmosphere, high contrast.

### 3. Lifestyle Scene (photography_person_lifestyle.png)
**Prompt:** Cyberpunk lifestyle photography, hands in tech-wear gloves interacting with a glowing mechanical object in a dark room. Harsh neon blue underlighting, gritty texture, deep black shadows, dystopian tech aesthetic.

### 4. Ecommerce Product (photography_product_ecommerce.png)
**Prompt:** Cyberpunk product photography, a dark, complex technological object resting on a wet concrete surface. Illuminated by a single harsh neon magenta light, deep black background, gritty, utilitarian and futuristic.

### 5. Product Detail (photography_product_detail.png)
**Prompt:** Cyberpunk macro photography, extreme close-up of dark metallic circuitry or industrial tech texture. Glowing neon cyan lights reflecting off the wet metal, harsh lighting, gritty high-contrast shadows.

### 6. Product in Use (photography_product_in_use.png)
**Prompt:** Cyberpunk product-in-use photography, a person using a glowing futuristic device in the dark. The device casts harsh neon light onto their face, deep black shadows surrounding them, dystopian hacker aesthetic.

### 7. Place Exterior (photography_place_exterior.png)
**Prompt:** Cyberpunk architectural exterior, a massive, dark, utilitarian skyscraper stretching into a smoggy night sky. Illuminated by giant glowing neon holograms and harsh industrial lights, rain-slicked, dystopian scale.

### 8. Place Interior (photography_place_interior.png)
**Prompt:** Cyberpunk interior photography, a dark, cramped industrial room filled with exposed wires and glowing screens. Lit entirely by harsh neon pink and blue light, deep oppressive shadows, gritty and utilitarian.

### 9. Place Detail (photography_place_detail.png)
**Prompt:** Cyberpunk architectural detail, close crop of wet, rough concrete and exposed steel pipes illuminated by a harsh neon light tube. Gritty texture, high contrast, dark futuristic industrial aesthetic.

### 10. Abstract Atmosphere (photography_abstract.png)
**Prompt:** Cyberpunk abstract photography, a pure study of neon magenta and cyan lights reflecting off rain-slicked black asphalt. High contrast, gritty texture, deep pure black shadows, dystopian night atmosphere.
