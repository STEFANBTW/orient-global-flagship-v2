# Image Generation Prompts for Psychedelic / Surrealist

If you run out of image generation quota, use these 10 highly-tailored Midjourney/DALL-E prompts to generate the photography assets for this aesthetic.

---

### 1. Person Portrait (photography_person_portrait.png)
**Prompt:** Psychedelic surreal portrait photography, subject's face distorted or overlaid with fluid, melting colors. Trippy, hallucinatory lighting, vivid hyper-saturated colors, dreamlike and disorienting aesthetic, highly imaginative.

### 2. Person in Environment (photography_person_environment.png)
**Prompt:** Psychedelic surreal environmental photography, a person in a bizarre, dreamlike landscape where gravity seems broken. Vivid clashing colors, strange proportions, hallucinatory lighting, mind-bending and surreal.

### 3. Lifestyle Scene (photography_person_lifestyle.png)
**Prompt:** Psychedelic surreal lifestyle photography, interaction with melting or floating objects in an impossible room. Vivid, hyper-saturated lighting, strange optical illusions, dreamlike, mind-bending visual experience.

### 4. Ecommerce Product (photography_product_ecommerce.png)
**Prompt:** Psychedelic surreal product photography, an everyday object that appears to be melting or twisting, resting on a wavy, intensely colorful background. Hallucinatory lighting, fluid shapes, trippy and imaginative.

### 5. Product Detail (photography_product_detail.png)
**Prompt:** Psychedelic surreal macro photography, extreme close-up of a fluid, morphing texture in hyper-saturated neon colors. Dreamlike depth of field, disorienting patterns, highly imaginative, mind-bending.

### 6. Product in Use (photography_product_in_use.png)
**Prompt:** Psychedelic surreal product-in-use photography, hands with impossible proportions interacting with a glowing, floating object. Vivid trippy colors, hallucinatory lighting, defying physical laws, dreamlike.

### 7. Place Exterior (photography_place_exterior.png)
**Prompt:** Psychedelic surreal architectural exterior, a building that appears to be melting or constructed of impossible geometries, set against a vividly colored, swirling sky. Dreamlike, hallucinatory, mind-bending.

### 8. Place Interior (photography_place_interior.png)
**Prompt:** Psychedelic surreal interior photography, a room where the floor curves into the ceiling, filled with fluid, brightly colored light. Impossible physics, highly imaginative, disorienting and trippy.

### 9. Place Detail (photography_place_detail.png)
**Prompt:** Psychedelic surreal architectural detail, close crop of walls or doors that are warping and melting into vivid puddles of color. Dreamlike lighting, defying physics, hallucinatory and highly imaginative.

### 10. Abstract Atmosphere (photography_abstract.png)
**Prompt:** Psychedelic surreal abstract photography, a pure study of fluid, melting neon colors swirling together in an impossible space. Trippy, hallucinatory, hyper-saturated, deeply disorienting and imaginative.
