  • Swiss International Design System
    • 🏛️ Ranked Industries
      • 1. **Financial Technology (FinTech) Platforms**: the precision, neutrality, and systematic clarity of Swiss design are perfectly aligned with financial data presentation
      • 2. **News & Information Journalism**: the International Style was literally developed for newspaper and magazine information design
      • 3. **Government & Public Sector Communications**: neutral, accessible, and systematically clear for diverse populations
      • 4. **Transportation & Wayfinding Systems**: Swiss design created the visual language of transit signage (the London Underground, the New York subway system)
      • 5. **Medical Devices & Healthcare Technology**: precision and clarity in life-critical information contexts
      • 6. **Academic Publishing & Research Institutions**: the communication of complex knowledge with maximal clarity
      • 7. **Data Visualization Products**: the systematic grid and typographic clarity of Swiss design are native to information graphics
      • 8. **Architecture Firms (Rationalist)**: the design philosophy of rational construction and systematic organization
      • 9. **Premium Consumer Electronics (Clean Utility)**: brands whose entire proposition is refined, purposeful function
      • 10. **International Organizations & Multilateral Institutions**: the UN, WHO, and similar bodies use the International Style because it transcends national visual conventions
      • 11. **The Deterministic Application Guide**: Swiss / International Style

    • ⚠️ Strict Avoids
      • **More than one accent color**: the system is absolute. One color serves identity and priority marking. Two colors create a hierarchy ambiguity.
      • **Centered body text**: flush left is a functional requirement, not a stylistic preference. Centered body text violates the legibility principles the style is built on.
      • **Justified body text — for the same reason**: irregular word spacing disrupts reading
      • **Decorative typography**: any font other than a neutral grotesque (Helvetica-family) for primary text violates the systematic neutrality
      • **Rounded corners**: they communicate approachability, which is a personal expression, which is inadmissible in the International Style
      • **Inconsistent grid adherence**: one element that does not sit on the baseline grid disrupts the invisible systematic harmony of the entire composition
      • **Gradients or textures**: the surface is flat, white, and neutral. The information organizes itself against this neutral ground.

Design Manifesto

We build digital instruments. Every scroll should feel weighted, every motion considered.

By Antigravity — July 6, 2026

When you strip a design down to only what is essential, you eliminate visual friction entirely. The brain processes the page effortlessly. That effortlessness feels like quality. Humans associate ease of processing with truth, trustworthiness, and beauty.
