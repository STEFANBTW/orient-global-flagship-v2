# Image Generation Prompts for Neumorphism

If you run out of image generation quota, use these 10 highly-tailored Midjourney/DALL-E prompts to generate the photography assets for this aesthetic.

---

### 1. Person Portrait (photography_person_portrait.png)
**Prompt:** Neumorphism portrait photography, subject wearing smooth matte clothing, extremely soft low-contrast lighting, pure off-white background, pristine and calm, soft unified color palette.

### 2. Person in Environment (photography_person_environment.png)
**Prompt:** Neumorphism environmental portrait, a person in a vast, pristine white room with soft curved corners, incredibly diffused light creating soft continuous shadows, calm, ultra-minimalist, matte surfaces.

### 3. Lifestyle Scene (photography_person_lifestyle.png)
**Prompt:** Neumorphism lifestyle photography, interaction with soft matte objects, seamless monochromatic white or light gray setting, highly diffused light, pristine, relaxing, absolutely no harsh shadows.

### 4. Ecommerce Product (photography_product_ecommerce.png)
**Prompt:** Neumorphism product photography, a smooth matte white object on a seamless white background, lit with soft omnidirectional light, smooth continuous gradients of shadow and highlight, pristine tech feel.

### 5. Product Detail (photography_product_detail.png)
**Prompt:** Neumorphism macro photography, extreme close-up of a seamless matte surface blending into the background, soft bevels, completely smooth texture, incredibly soft shadow gradients, calm and pristine.

### 6. Product in Use (photography_product_in_use.png)
**Prompt:** Neumorphism product-in-use photography, hands interacting with a smooth seamless matte object, low contrast, off-white monochromatic environment, ultra-soft diffused lighting, peaceful aesthetic.

### 7. Place Exterior (photography_place_exterior.png)
**Prompt:** Neumorphism architectural exterior, a futuristic building with seamless curved matte white walls, soft ambient sky lighting, no harsh edges, feeling like it is extruded from the ground, tranquil.

### 8. Place Interior (photography_place_interior.png)
**Prompt:** Neumorphism interior photography, a pristine white space with curved walls and seamless built-in matte furniture, soft glowing indirect light, absolute calm, low-contrast shadows, futuristic wellness space.

### 9. Place Detail (photography_place_detail.png)
**Prompt:** Neumorphism architectural detail, close crop of a smooth curved white wall meeting a flat surface with a soft continuous shadow, completely matte, monochromatic, study in subtle lighting.

### 10. Abstract Atmosphere (photography_abstract.png)
**Prompt:** Neumorphism abstract photography, a study of soft matte geometric forms blending into a seamless background, incredibly soft low-contrast lighting, pristine off-white tones, calm continuous gradients.
