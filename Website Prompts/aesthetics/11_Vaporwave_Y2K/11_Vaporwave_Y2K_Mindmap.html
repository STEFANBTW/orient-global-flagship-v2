  • Vaporwave Y2K Design System
    • 🏛️ Ranked Industries
      • 1. **Independent Music Artists & Record Labels**: vaporwave is literally a music genre first; its visual language was born from music culture
      • 2. **Streetwear & Fashion (Youth-Oriented)**: Y2K fashion has returned as a dominant trend; the aesthetic is the market's native language
      • 3. **Gaming (Indie / Retro-Inspired)**: indie games referencing the 8-bit and 16-bit eras use this aesthetic as cultural shorthand
      • 4. **Beauty & Cosmetics (Gen Z Brands)**: holographic packaging, iridescent products, and Y2K-inspired campaigns dominate Gen Z beauty
      • 5. **Social Media Tools & Creator Platforms**: platforms built for Gen Z creators speak their visual language
      • 6. **Entertainment & Pop Culture Media**: nostalgia-driven entertainment companies referencing the 1990s and 2000s
      • 7. **Food & Beverage (Novelty / Youth Market)**: brands selling experience and personality alongside product
      • 8. **Event Promotion (Themed Events, Raves, Festivals)**: the aesthetic is native to late-night event culture
      • 9. **Tech Accessories & Consumer Electronics**: holographic cases, retro-styled gadgets, nostalgic hardware
      • 10. **Art & Design Studios (Self-Promotion)**: designers and artists referencing retro digital culture as their creative territory
      • 11. **The Deterministic Application Guide**: Vaporwave / Y2K / Retro-Futurism

    • ⚠️ Strict Avoids
      • **Neutral, unsaturated colors**: vaporwave's palette is its defining characteristic; desaturated tones destroy it entirely
      • **Minimalist layouts**: the aesthetic's richness requires layering; minimal white space reads as wrong
      • **Contemporary sans-serif only typography**: the retro font references are essential to the aesthetic's cultural coding
      • **Clean, realistic photography**: unprocessed contemporary photography is visually inconsistent with the degraded, nostalgic imagery the aesthetic requires
      • **Flat, Google Material-style icons**: use pixel art icons, retro-styled icons, or 3D primitive icons
      • **Single solid background colors**: gradients are mandatory; solid backgrounds feel empty and wrong
      • **Taking itself too seriously**: vaporwave has an inherent irony and self-awareness. A vaporwave design that is earnestly solemn has missed the point entirely.

Design Manifesto

We build digital instruments. Every scroll should feel weighted, every motion considered.

By Antigravity — July 6, 2026

When you strip a design down to only what is essential, you eliminate visual friction entirely. The brain processes the page effortlessly. That effortlessness feels like quality. Humans associate ease of processing with truth, trustworthiness, and beauty.
