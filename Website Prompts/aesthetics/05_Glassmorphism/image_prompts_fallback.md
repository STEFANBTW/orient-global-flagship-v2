# Image Generation Prompts for Glassmorphism

If you run out of image generation quota, use these 10 highly-tailored Midjourney/DALL-E prompts to generate the photography assets for this aesthetic.

---

### 1. Person Portrait (photography_person_portrait.png)
**Prompt:** Glassmorphism portrait photography, close-up, subject seen partially through a pane of frosted glass, soft glowing pastel neon light, ethereal atmosphere, translucent dreamlike quality.

### 2. Person in Environment (photography_person_environment.png)
**Prompt:** Glassmorphism environmental photography, silhouette of a person in a modern space made entirely of translucent frosted glass and light, holographic pastel gradients, soft ambient lighting.

### 3. Lifestyle Scene (photography_person_lifestyle.png)
**Prompt:** Glassmorphism lifestyle photography, elegant interaction with clear and frosted acrylic objects, soft glowing pastel background blur (bokeh), futuristic but delicate, high-key lighting.

### 4. Ecommerce Product (photography_product_ecommerce.png)
**Prompt:** Glassmorphism product photography, a translucent object resting on frosted glass, colorful pastel gradient light shining through it, soft reflections, ethereal, incredibly clean and modern.

### 5. Product Detail (photography_product_detail.png)
**Prompt:** Glassmorphism macro photography, extreme close-up of frosted glass texture, a gradient of pink and blue light refracting through the translucent edge, soft blur, dreamy.

### 6. Product in Use (photography_product_in_use.png)
**Prompt:** Glassmorphism product-in-use photography, hands holding a transparent tech device, the background heavily blurred in pastel tones, soft light refraction, glowing edges, futuristic elegance.

### 7. Place Exterior (photography_place_exterior.png)
**Prompt:** Glassmorphism architectural exterior, modern pavilion made of curved frosted and dichroic glass, reflecting pastel sky colors, translucent layers, soft atmospheric lighting.

### 8. Place Interior (photography_place_interior.png)
**Prompt:** Glassmorphism interior photography, a pristine room partitioned by fluted glass screens, soft pastel gradient lights glowing from behind the glass, ethereal modern space, highly diffused light.

### 9. Place Detail (photography_place_detail.png)
**Prompt:** Glassmorphism architectural detail, close crop of layered translucent glass panels, light bending and refracting into soft pastel rainbows, clean minimalist aesthetic, high-end tech feel.

### 10. Abstract Atmosphere (photography_abstract.png)
**Prompt:** Glassmorphism abstract photography, a pure study of frosted glass layers and depth of field, blurred background showing soft glowing pastel light, dreamlike, translucent aesthetics.
