# 01. MINIMALISM - Design Specification Document

This document extracts and semantically formats the strict design specifications for the **MINIMALISM** aesthetic, from the ranked industries to the critical guidelines on what to strictly avoid.

---

## 1. Ranked Industries
Below are the industries most to least suited for the Minimalism design style:

| Rank | Industry / Domain | Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Luxury Watchmaking** | Minimalism is the language of high-end watches; restraint signals precision and exclusivity. |
| **2** | **Premium SaaS / Productivity Software** | Clean interfaces reduce cognitive load; perfection feels expensive. |
| **3** | **High-End Interior Design / Architecture** | Minimalism is the founding philosophy of modern interior design. |
| **4** | **Luxury Fashion / Haute Couture** | White space on a runway, as on a website, signals confidence and exclusivity. |
| **5** | **Medical / Healthcare Tech** | Clarity and calmness are critical; minimalism reduces anxiety in clinical contexts. |
| **6** | **Fine Art Galleries / Museums** | The content (art) should dominate; the site interface should disappear. |
| **7** | **Premium Skincare / Cosmetics** | Ingredients and simplicity are selling points; overcomplicated visuals undermine the message. |
| **8** | **Legal / Consultancy Services** | Trust is built on clarity; minimalism communicates that you are in control and do not need to oversell yourself. |
| **9** | **Financial Planning / Wealth Management** | Money is serious; clutter creates anxiety. Clean design communicates stability and competence. |
| **10** | **Independent Photography Studios** | The photographs are the product; the website must be an invisible frame around them. |

---

## 2. Psychological Foundation
* **Cognitive Load Reduction:** When a page is dense with competing elements, the brain spends energy deciding what to process first. Stripping design down to essentials eliminates visual friction. Humans associate ease of processing with truth, trustworthiness, and beauty.
* **Scarcity Signals Value:** Generous whitespace is expensive. A brand that can afford to display almost nothing communicates ultimate confidence in the value of its select content, acting as a luxury signal.

## 4. Typography
In minimalism, typography **is** the design. Never mix more than two font families.

* **Path A — The Geometric Sans-Serif:** (e.g. *Inter*, *Futura*, *DM Sans*). communicate efficiency and mathematical harmony. Best for tech, software, finance, and medical.
* **Path B — The Refined Serif:** (e.g. *Garamond*, *Cormorant Garamond*, *Playfair Display*, *Didot*). communicate historical authority and timelessness. Best for luxury fashion, art, jewelry, and hospitality.

### Sizing and Spacing Scale
* **Display Headline (H1):** `72px` to `96px` (Line height: `1.0` to `1.15`). Sans-Serif headlines should use slightly tighter letter spacing (`-0.02em` to `-0.04em`).
* **Section Headline (H2):** `40px` to `52px`.
* **Sub-Heading (H3):** `24px` to `30px`.
* **Body Text:** `17px` to `19px` (Line height: `1.7` to `1.9`). Never bold body text.
* **Caption / Label:** `11px` to `13px` (Uppercase, with wide letter tracking of `0.12em` to `0.18em`).

---

## 5. Spacing & Layout
* **Whitespace Philosophy:** Space represents visual importance.
* **Section Padding:** `120px` to `160px` vertical padding for desktop (`64px` to `80px` for mobile).
* **Content Max-Width:**
  * **Text-heavy content:** `680px` to `760px` (optimizes reading width).
  * **Visual blocks / grids:** `1200px` to `1280px` maximum.
* **Alignment Rule:** Left-aligned or centered—never mix both on the same page.
* **Layout Structure:** Prefers single-column layouts. If using two columns, use a massive column gap (`80px` to `120px`).

---

## 6. Borders, Shapes & Forms
* **Sharp Corner Geometry:** Precison requires care.
  * **Cards & Containers:** `0px` to `4px` border radius.
  * **Buttons & Inputs:** `4px` to `8px` border radius.
* **Borders:** Hairline (1px) dividers in light grey (`#E0E0E0`) used only for vertical separation. No four-sided border cages around content cards.
* **Shadows:** Avoid shadows. If necessary, use almost invisible depth: `box-shadow: 0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.06)`.
* **Forms:** Underline style inputs (bottom border only) transitioning from `#CCCCCC` at rest to `#1A1A1A` on focus. Red error feedback color: `#C0392B`.

---

## 7. UI Components
* **Navigation (Navbar):** Transparent at top. Clean text logo on the left (regular weight), simple text links on the right (no uppercase/underlines). Hover triggers simple color shift only.
* **Primary Button:** Foreground background (`#1A1A1A`), off-white text (`#FAFAFA`). Hover subtly lightens background to `#333333`.
* **Secondary Button:** Transparent background, thin foreground border, foreground text. Inverts colors on hover.
* **Cards:** No border or shadow structures. Content floats freely, grouped with large padding or subtle `#F5F5F5` background shifts.

---

## 8. Imagery, Texture & Animation
* **Photography:** Desaturated, singular subjects, high negative space, studio-diffused light.
* **Texture:** Barely-perceptible noise overlay (3% to 5% opacity) to prevent digital sterility.
* **Motion & Transitions:**
  * Clean opacity fades (`500ms` to `700ms`) on scroll.
  * Ease curves must be `ease-out`. Hover transition speed is `200ms` to `250ms`.
  * Stagger animations must be fast (`60ms` to `80ms` gap).

---

## 9. What to Strictly Avoid in Minimalism
1. Mix more than two font families.
2. Drop shadows on multiple elements.
3. Saturated color backgrounds or gradients.
4. Decorative icon packs (use only uniform, single-stroke custom lines).
5. Multiple Call-To-Action (CTA) buttons per section.
6. Centered and left-aligned text mixed on the same page.
7. Four-sided borders or solid cages around cards.
8. Mechanical bouncing, spring physics, or text-parallax animations.
