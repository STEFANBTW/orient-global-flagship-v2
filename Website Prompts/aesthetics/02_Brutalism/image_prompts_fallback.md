# Image Generation Prompts for Brutalism

If you run out of image generation quota, use these 10 highly-tailored Midjourney/DALL-E prompts to generate the photography assets for this aesthetic.

---

### 1. Person Portrait (photography_person_portrait.png)
**Prompt:** Portrait photography of a single person, close-frame. raw, unretouched, high contrast, black and white or jarring colors, no softening, harsh flash, architectural concrete feel. No props, no background distraction. Ultra high-end photography feel.

### 2. Person in Environment (photography_person_environment.png)
**Prompt:** Full figure of a person standing in a vast architectural space. The person is small relative to the frame, emphasizing scale and negative space. raw, unretouched, high contrast, black and white or jarring colors, no softening, harsh flash, architectural concrete feel. Even, diffused natural light.

### 3. Lifestyle Scene (photography_person_lifestyle.png)
**Prompt:** Lifestyle photography: a person interacting with a single clean object in an interior. The scene is uncluttered. raw, unretouched, high contrast, black and white or jarring colors, no softening, harsh flash, architectural concrete feel. Soft natural window light from one side. Editorial luxury magazine feel.

### 4. Ecommerce Product (photography_product_ecommerce.png)
**Prompt:** Ecommerce product photography: a single elegant object centered on a studio background. perfectly even lighting. raw, unretouched, high contrast, black and white or jarring colors, no softening, harsh flash, architectural concrete feel. Maximum negative space. The image feels like a luxury brand's product page.

### 5. Product Detail (photography_product_detail.png)
**Prompt:** Macro product detail photography: an extreme close-up of a single material surface or object edge. raw, unretouched, high contrast, black and white or jarring colors, no softening, harsh flash, architectural concrete feel. Soft even light. The frame focuses on one singular texture.

### 6. Product in Use (photography_product_in_use.png)
**Prompt:** Product-in-use photography: human hands holding or using a simple, elegant object. raw, unretouched, high contrast, black and white or jarring colors, no softening, harsh flash, architectural concrete feel. Diffused soft studio light. The scene communicates quietude.

### 7. Place Exterior (photography_place_exterior.png)
**Prompt:** Architectural exterior photography: a clean building facade or exterior, photographed straight-on. raw, unretouched, high contrast, black and white or jarring colors, no softening, harsh flash, architectural concrete feel. No people, no cars. Vast sky above. The frame is mostly negative space.

### 8. Place Interior (photography_place_interior.png)
**Prompt:** Interior space photography: an immaculate room. raw, unretouched, high contrast, black and white or jarring colors, no softening, harsh flash, architectural concrete feel. One or two pieces of simple furniture. Natural light from a large window. No decoration, no clutter.

### 9. Place Detail (photography_place_detail.png)
**Prompt:** Architectural detail photography: a close crop of where two surfaces meet. Abstract geometric composition. raw, unretouched, high contrast, black and white or jarring colors, no softening, harsh flash, architectural concrete feel. Even soft light. No people.

### 10. Abstract Atmosphere (photography_abstract.png)
**Prompt:** Abstract atmosphere photography: light and shadow study. raw, unretouched, high contrast, black and white or jarring colors, no softening, harsh flash, architectural concrete feel. A beam of soft light crossing a surface. The image is a study of negative space and the edge of light.
