        • Strict rigid 12-column layouts without optical alignment
        • No Grid at all (arbitrary CSS absolute X/Y placements)
      • Spacing Attributes
        • Inconsistent Section Padding (breaks scroll rhythm)
        • Content pushed to extreme edges touching border borders
      • Launch Spacing & Borders Studio
    • ⬡ Borders & Shapes
      • Border Rules
        • Thick solid black/white box cages (3px to 6px solid)
        • Full-page outer frame borders (8px to 12px solid)
        • Asymmetric borders (thick left/bottom only)
      • Border Radius
        • Locked to 0px (No softening allowed)
      • Shapes & Graphics
        • Raw rectangles and squares as backdrop blocks
        • No organic blobs, curves, or soft shapes
        • No drop shadows (flat offset 4px 4px shadow is OK, no blur)
    • ▢ UI Components
      • Navigation
        • Thick borders dividing horizontal menu links
        • Vertical left-side header column with thick right border
        • Instant hover inverts (no transition, 0ms)
      • Buttons
        • Solid blocks of pure accent color with thick black outline
        • ALL CAPS heavy typography, 0px border radius
        • 0ms instant hover color-inverting
      • Cards
        • Thick border cages (3px-5px), minimal padding (12px-16px)
        • Stacked overlapping cards layers
      • Launch UI Components Sandbox
    • 🖼️ Imagery & Texture
      • Photography Rules
        • Raw unretouched documentary style
        • Desaturated high contrast black and white prints
        • Unconventional cropping (cut off heads, extreme closeups)
      • Textures & Patterns
        • Halftone newspaper dots overlay
        • Risograph misaligned printing texturing
        • Noise/grain overlay at high opacity (20% to 40%)
      • Illustration & Collage
        • Punk flyer zine cut-and-paste collage details
        • Rough sketchy hand-drawn line work
        • Flat retro pixel artwork
      • Launch Imagery & Texture Lab
    • ⚡ Animation & Motion
      • Allowed Interactions
        • Instant state changes (0ms hover/active swaps)
        • Infinite news scrolling marquees
        • Glitch distortion and digital splitting displacement
        • Blinking terminal block cursor
      • Animation Avoids
        • No smooth eased hover transitions
        • No parallax scrolling layouts
        • No soft page fades (loads abruptly)
        • No spring physics (Framer Motion style)
      • Launch Animation Studio
    • ⚠️ Strict Avoids
      • No rounded corners anywhere (not even 1px)
      • No background gradients (only solid raw colors)
      • No soft Gaussian drop shadows
      • No color harmonies or muted lifestyle colors
      • No predictable scroll spacing rhythms
      • No color graded, retouched, beautiful studio photos
      • No light or thin font weights
      • No animation that delights or smooths UX
