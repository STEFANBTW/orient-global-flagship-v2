# Image Generation Prompts for Corporate / Professional

If you run out of image generation quota, use these 10 highly-tailored Midjourney/DALL-E prompts to generate the photography assets for this aesthetic.

---

### 1. Person Portrait (photography_person_portrait.png)
**Prompt:** Corporate professional portrait photography, subject in modern business attire, bright, clean, even studio lighting. Trustworthy, approachable, crisp focus, cool blue and neutral color tones, high-end corporate headshot.

### 2. Person in Environment (photography_person_environment.png)
**Prompt:** Corporate professional environmental portrait, a person in a bright, modern glass-and-steel office building. Crisp, clean natural light, professional atmosphere, cool tones, communicating efficiency and trust.

### 3. Lifestyle Scene (photography_person_lifestyle.png)
**Prompt:** Corporate professional lifestyle photography, people collaborating in a sleek modern boardroom. Bright, even lighting, clean aesthetic, cool blue tones, professional, dynamic but highly organized.

### 4. Ecommerce Product (photography_product_ecommerce.png)
**Prompt:** Corporate professional product photography, a clean, modern technological or office object on a crisp white or light grey background. Even, bright lighting, perfectly clean, trustworthy and highly functional.

### 5. Product Detail (photography_product_detail.png)
**Prompt:** Corporate professional macro photography, close-up of a sleek, modern device or architectural feature (like brushed aluminum). Crisp, bright lighting, clean lines, professional and high-quality manufacturing.

### 6. Product in Use (photography_product_in_use.png)
**Prompt:** Corporate professional product-in-use photography, hands interacting with a sleek modern laptop or device. Bright, clean lighting, organized desk space, professional, efficient, cool neutral color palette.

### 7. Place Exterior (photography_place_exterior.png)
**Prompt:** Corporate professional architectural exterior, a towering modern glass-and-steel office building against a clear blue sky. Bright, crisp sunlight, clean geometric lines, communicating stability, scale, and modern business.

### 8. Place Interior (photography_place_interior.png)
**Prompt:** Corporate professional interior photography, a bright, expansive modern office lobby or workspace. Glass walls, clean lines, bright even lighting, cool neutral tones, highly organized and professional atmosphere.

### 9. Place Detail (photography_place_detail.png)
**Prompt:** Corporate professional architectural detail, close crop of clean glass intersecting with brushed steel framing. Bright, crisp lighting, emphasizing precision, modern engineering, and corporate cleanliness.

### 10. Abstract Atmosphere (photography_abstract.png)
**Prompt:** Corporate professional abstract photography, a clean, bright study of light reflecting off modern architectural glass facades. Cool blue tones, crisp geometric lines, representing corporate efficiency and modern business.
