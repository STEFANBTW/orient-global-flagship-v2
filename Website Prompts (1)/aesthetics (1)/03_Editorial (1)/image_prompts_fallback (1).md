# Image Generation Prompts for Editorial

If you run out of image generation quota, use these 10 highly-tailored Midjourney/DALL-E prompts to generate the photography assets for this aesthetic.

---

### 1. Person Portrait (photography_person_portrait.png)
**Prompt:** Editorial magazine photography, portrait orientation, close-up of a person with subtle warm color grading, art-directed, narrative lighting, high-end journalism style, sophisticated.

### 2. Person in Environment (photography_person_environment.png)
**Prompt:** Editorial environmental portrait photography, full figure of a person in a visually rich real-world context, narrative story-telling, subtle cool color grading, magazine cover quality.

### 3. Lifestyle Scene (photography_person_lifestyle.png)
**Prompt:** Editorial lifestyle photography, narrative scene with subtle warm tones, elegant interaction, art-directed composition, sophisticated magazine spread feel.

### 4. Ecommerce Product (photography_product_ecommerce.png)
**Prompt:** Editorial product photography, an elegant object placed in a narrative still-life composition, subtle lighting, warm paper-like tones, art-directed, sophisticated.

### 5. Product Detail (photography_product_detail.png)
**Prompt:** Editorial macro photography, extreme close-up of fine print or refined texture on paper, subtle lighting, elegant depth of field, journalistic documentary feel.

### 6. Product in Use (photography_product_in_use.png)
**Prompt:** Editorial product-in-use photography, elegant hands holding a sophisticated object in a lifestyle context, narrative depth, magazine-style color grading, art-directed still life.

### 7. Place Exterior (photography_place_exterior.png)
**Prompt:** Editorial architectural photography, exterior of a classic or narratively interesting building, journalistic composition, subtle cinematic color grading, magazine feature story imagery.

### 8. Place Interior (photography_place_interior.png)
**Prompt:** Editorial interior photography, a culturally rich and sophisticated room with narrative details, natural lighting, subtle warm grading, capturing the mood of a journalistic profile.

### 9. Place Detail (photography_place_detail.png)
**Prompt:** Editorial architectural detail photography, close crop of an elegant architectural feature, documentary style, art-directed framing, sophisticated shadows.

### 10. Abstract Atmosphere (photography_abstract.png)
**Prompt:** Editorial abstract photography, a moody narrative study of light passing through glass or paper, subtle warm tones, sophisticated magazine aesthetic, high-end art direction.
