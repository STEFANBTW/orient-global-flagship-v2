# Image Generation Prompts for Skeuomorphism

If you run out of image generation quota, use these 10 highly-tailored Midjourney/DALL-E prompts to generate the photography assets for this aesthetic.

---

### 1. Person Portrait (photography_person_portrait.png)
**Prompt:** Skeuomorphism portrait photography, close-up, subject wearing rich tactile materials like leather or heavy wool, dramatic lighting highlighting deep textures, realistic, warm ambient lighting, tangible aesthetic.

### 2. Person in Environment (photography_person_environment.png)
**Prompt:** Skeuomorphism environmental photography, a person in a space filled with rich, realistic textures—dark wood panels, stitched leather, and brushed metal. Warm directional lighting casting realistic shadows, highly tactile environment.

### 3. Lifestyle Scene (photography_person_lifestyle.png)
**Prompt:** Skeuomorphism lifestyle photography, hands interacting with beautifully crafted analog objects, rich warm lighting, hyper-realistic details, highlighting physical buttons, dials, and luxurious tangible materials.

### 4. Ecommerce Product (photography_product_ecommerce.png)
**Prompt:** Skeuomorphism product photography, an analog object featuring rich wood grain, polished metal bevels, and stitched leather, resting on a textured surface. Dramatic lighting highlighting specular highlights and deep cast shadows, tangible and heavy.

### 5. Product Detail (photography_product_detail.png)
**Prompt:** Skeuomorphism macro photography, extreme close-up of a physical dial, stitched leather, and brushed metal interface. Hyper-realistic, dramatic specular highlights, rich depth, emphasizing the tactile physical nature of the object.

### 6. Product in Use (photography_product_in_use.png)
**Prompt:** Skeuomorphism product-in-use photography, fingers turning a physical metallic dial or pressing a glossy mechanical button. Rich warm lighting, extremely detailed textures, highlighting physical interaction.

### 7. Place Exterior (photography_place_exterior.png)
**Prompt:** Skeuomorphism architectural photography, a building showcasing rich physical materials like raw timber, heavy stone, and polished brass. Warm afternoon sunlight casting dramatic and highly realistic shadows, emphasizing tangible materials.

### 8. Place Interior (photography_place_interior.png)
**Prompt:** Skeuomorphism interior photography, a study or library featuring heavy dark wood desks, green glass lamps, and leather armchairs. Rich, warm directional lighting, highlighting the physical reality of analog materials.

### 9. Place Detail (photography_place_detail.png)
**Prompt:** Skeuomorphism architectural detail, close crop of polished brass hardware mounted on rich grained mahogany wood. Dramatic lighting emphasizing reflections, bevels, and the heavy tactile nature of the materials.

### 10. Abstract Atmosphere (photography_abstract.png)
**Prompt:** Skeuomorphism abstract photography, an extreme macro study of stitched leather meeting brushed steel. Warm lighting catching the specular highlights of the metal and the deep grain of the leather, hyper-realistic, tangible.
