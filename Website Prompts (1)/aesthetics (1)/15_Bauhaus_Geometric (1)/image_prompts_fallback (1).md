# Image Generation Prompts for Bauhaus / Geometric

If you run out of image generation quota, use these 10 highly-tailored Midjourney/DALL-E prompts to generate the photography assets for this aesthetic.

---

### 1. Person Portrait (photography_person_portrait.png)
**Prompt:** Bauhaus geometric portrait photography, subject wearing highly structured, architectural clothing in primary colors. Stark, clean studio lighting, highly analytical composition, bold geometry, functional aesthetic.

### 2. Person in Environment (photography_person_environment.png)
**Prompt:** Bauhaus geometric environmental photography, a person in an ultra-functional, minimalist room featuring bold primary colors (red, blue, yellow) and harsh geometric shapes. Crisp lighting, highly analytical and stark.

### 3. Lifestyle Scene (photography_person_lifestyle.png)
**Prompt:** Bauhaus geometric lifestyle photography, interaction with highly functional, geometric objects (spheres, cylinders, cubes). Primary color palette, harsh distinct shadows, function over form, analytical composition.

### 4. Ecommerce Product (photography_product_ecommerce.png)
**Prompt:** Bauhaus geometric product photography, a stark, purely functional object composed of simple geometric shapes, resting on a flat color background. Crisp directional light, primary colors, zero ornamentation.

### 5. Product Detail (photography_product_detail.png)
**Prompt:** Bauhaus geometric macro photography, extreme close-up of a functional joint or intersection of pure geometric shapes (a circle meeting a straight line). Crisp, analytical lighting, primary color accents.

### 6. Product in Use (photography_product_in_use.png)
**Prompt:** Bauhaus geometric product-in-use photography, hands interacting with an object whose design is entirely dictated by its function. Stark, clean lighting, pure geometric composition, primary colors, analytical.

### 7. Place Exterior (photography_place_exterior.png)
**Prompt:** Bauhaus geometric architectural exterior, a building constructed of pure, unadorned geometric volumes (cubes and cylinders). Stark white walls with primary color accents, harsh sunlight casting sharp shadows, entirely functional.

### 8. Place Interior (photography_place_interior.png)
**Prompt:** Bauhaus geometric interior photography, a highly functional room stripped of all decoration. Pure geometric furniture (tubular steel and canvas), primary colored walls or rugs, stark clean light, analytical.

### 9. Place Detail (photography_place_detail.png)
**Prompt:** Bauhaus geometric architectural detail, close crop of a purely functional, unadorned structural element—like a tubular steel handrail meeting a flat concrete wall. Stark lighting, geometric precision.

### 10. Abstract Atmosphere (photography_abstract.png)
**Prompt:** Bauhaus geometric abstract photography, a pure study of primary colors (red, blue, yellow) and stark geometric shadows (circles, triangles, squares) interacting on a flat surface. Analytical, functional, unembellished.
