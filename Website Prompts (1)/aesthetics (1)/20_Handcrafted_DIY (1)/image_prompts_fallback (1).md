# Image Generation Prompts for Handcrafted / DIY

If you run out of image generation quota, use these 10 highly-tailored Midjourney/DALL-E prompts to generate the photography assets for this aesthetic.

---

### 1. Person Portrait (photography_person_portrait.png)
**Prompt:** Handcrafted DIY portrait photography, subject in a messy artist studio or workshop, wearing an apron. Warm, natural window light, authentic, unpolished, earthy tones, highly approachable and human.

### 2. Person in Environment (photography_person_environment.png)
**Prompt:** Handcrafted DIY environmental photography, a person working in a cluttered, authentic craft studio filled with raw materials and tools. Warm, messy, organic lighting, earthy, highly approachable.

### 3. Lifestyle Scene (photography_person_lifestyle.png)
**Prompt:** Handcrafted DIY lifestyle photography, messy hands actively crafting, painting, or sculpting. A workspace filled with torn paper, paint splatters, and raw wood. Warm, authentic natural lighting, analogue feel.

### 4. Ecommerce Product (photography_product_ecommerce.png)
**Prompt:** Handcrafted DIY product photography, an imperfect, handmade object resting on a textured, messy workbench. Natural, warm lighting, showcasing the authentic imperfections and human touch, highly approachable.

### 5. Product Detail (photography_product_detail.png)
**Prompt:** Handcrafted DIY macro photography, extreme close-up of torn paper edges, rough ceramics, or visible brushstrokes. Warm, soft natural light emphasizing the beautiful imperfections of handmade crafts, earthy texture.

### 6. Product in Use (photography_product_in_use.png)
**Prompt:** Handcrafted DIY product-in-use photography, hands covered in clay or paint interacting with a raw, handmade object. Warm, messy studio environment, authentic, human, unpolished and highly approachable.

### 7. Place Exterior (photography_place_exterior.png)
**Prompt:** Handcrafted DIY architectural exterior, an eclectic, handmade cabin or messy artist studio built from reclaimed materials. Warm, inviting sunlight, highly authentic, imperfect, deeply human aesthetic.

### 8. Place Interior (photography_place_interior.png)
**Prompt:** Handcrafted DIY interior photography, a warm, inviting, messy studio space filled with raw craft materials, torn paper, and unpolished wood furniture. Soft, authentic natural light, deeply approachable and earthy.

### 9. Place Detail (photography_place_detail.png)
**Prompt:** Handcrafted DIY architectural detail, close crop of reclaimed, imperfect wood or a messy, paint-splattered surface. Warm natural lighting highlighting the authentic, analogue history of the materials.

### 10. Abstract Atmosphere (photography_abstract.png)
**Prompt:** Handcrafted DIY abstract photography, a messy, authentic study of overlapping torn paper textures, paint splatters, and raw canvas. Warm, earthy tones, deeply analogue, showcasing the beauty of imperfection.
