# Image Generation Prompts for Swiss International Style

If you run out of image generation quota, use these 10 highly-tailored Midjourney/DALL-E prompts to generate the photography assets for this aesthetic.

---

### 1. Person Portrait (photography_person_portrait.png)
**Prompt:** Swiss International Style portrait photography, subject photographed objectively against a pure white background. Flat, even lighting, highly neutral composition, completely unembellished, clinical, analytical, documentary style.

### 2. Person in Environment (photography_person_environment.png)
**Prompt:** Swiss International Style environmental photography, a person in a highly organized, grid-aligned space. Photographed straight-on, pure objective composition, neutral color palette, absolute clarity, no distortion, analytical.

### 3. Lifestyle Scene (photography_person_lifestyle.png)
**Prompt:** Swiss International Style lifestyle photography, interaction with highly functional, perfectly aligned objects on a grid layout desk. Objective, flat lighting, clinical cleanliness, pure white background, unembellished.

### 4. Ecommerce Product (photography_product_ecommerce.png)
**Prompt:** Swiss International Style product photography, a functional object perfectly centered on a pure white background. Flat, perfectly even studio lighting, objective representation, completely unembellished, clear and legible.

### 5. Product Detail (photography_product_detail.png)
**Prompt:** Swiss International Style macro photography, extreme close-up of a highly organized, functional mechanism or texture. Perfectly focused, objective, neutral lighting, communicating absolute clarity and precision.

### 6. Product in Use (photography_product_in_use.png)
**Prompt:** Swiss International Style product-in-use photography, hands demonstrating the objective function of an object. Pure white background, flat even lighting, no artistic embellishment, purely instructional and clear.

### 7. Place Exterior (photography_place_exterior.png)
**Prompt:** Swiss International Style architectural exterior, a highly functional, grid-based modern building. Photographed perfectly straight-on to remove perspective distortion, neutral overcast sky, objective and analytical representation.

### 8. Place Interior (photography_place_interior.png)
**Prompt:** Swiss International Style interior photography, an ultra-clean, highly organized space governed by strict grid alignments. Pure white walls, neutral lighting, objective composition, absolute clarity and order.

### 9. Place Detail (photography_place_detail.png)
**Prompt:** Swiss International Style architectural detail, close crop of a perfect right-angle intersection in a functional building. Objective, flat lighting, no dramatic shadows, pure clarity, communicating precision engineering.

### 10. Abstract Atmosphere (photography_abstract.png)
**Prompt:** Swiss International Style abstract photography, an objective study of a perfect grid or alignment of simple objects on a pure white background. Completely flat lighting, zero artistic distortion, absolute structural clarity.
