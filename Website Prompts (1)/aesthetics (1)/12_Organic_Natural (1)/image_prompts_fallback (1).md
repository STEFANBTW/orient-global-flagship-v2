# Image Generation Prompts for Organic / Natural

If you run out of image generation quota, use these 10 highly-tailored Midjourney/DALL-E prompts to generate the photography assets for this aesthetic.

---

### 1. Person Portrait (photography_person_portrait.png)
**Prompt:** Organic natural portrait photography, subject wearing sustainable natural fibers like linen or organic cotton, soft diffused morning sunlight, earthy tones, calm and grounded aesthetic, natural skin texture.

### 2. Person in Environment (photography_person_environment.png)
**Prompt:** Organic natural environmental photography, a person in a serene interior space filled with natural light, plants, and raw wood elements. Earthy color palette, soft diffused sunlight, calm and grounding atmosphere.

### 3. Lifestyle Scene (photography_person_lifestyle.png)
**Prompt:** Organic natural lifestyle photography, a person interacting gently with natural materials (pottery, plants, or linen) in a sunlit room. Soft, warm diffused light, earthy tones, relaxing and slow-living aesthetic.

### 4. Ecommerce Product (photography_product_ecommerce.png)
**Prompt:** Organic natural product photography, an earthy, sustainable object resting on a raw stone or light wood surface. Soft, highly diffused natural window light, subtle warm shadows, calm and minimalist presentation.

### 5. Product Detail (photography_product_detail.png)
**Prompt:** Organic natural macro photography, extreme close-up of a raw natural texture—woven linen, rough stone, or unpolished wood. Soft, gentle natural light highlighting the organic imperfections and tactile quality.

### 6. Product in Use (photography_product_in_use.png)
**Prompt:** Organic natural product-in-use photography, gentle hands interacting with a beautifully crafted earthen object or botanical element. Soft, warm, diffused morning sunlight, calm and mindful atmosphere, earthy palette.

### 7. Place Exterior (photography_place_exterior.png)
**Prompt:** Organic natural architectural exterior, a modern sustainable building seamlessly integrated into a lush natural landscape. Featuring raw wood and stone, soft golden-hour sunlight, harmonious and grounded.

### 8. Place Interior (photography_place_interior.png)
**Prompt:** Organic natural interior photography, a serene, sun-drenched room with raw wood floors, linen furniture, and abundant indoor plants. Soft diffused light creating a peaceful, grounding sanctuary.

### 9. Place Detail (photography_place_detail.png)
**Prompt:** Organic natural architectural detail, close crop of raw timber meeting natural stone. Soft, warm sunlight illuminating the organic textures and imperfections, wabi-sabi aesthetic, calm and grounding.

### 10. Abstract Atmosphere (photography_abstract.png)
**Prompt:** Organic natural abstract photography, a study of soft dappled sunlight filtering through leaves onto a raw linen or stone surface. Earthy warm tones, deeply calming, slow-living aesthetic.
