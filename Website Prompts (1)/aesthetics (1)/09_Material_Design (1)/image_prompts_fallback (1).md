# Image Generation Prompts for Material Design

If you run out of image generation quota, use these 10 highly-tailored Midjourney/DALL-E prompts to generate the photography assets for this aesthetic.

---

### 1. Person Portrait (photography_person_portrait.png)
**Prompt:** Material Design portrait photography, subject against a clean, bright, primary-color background. Sharp, intentional directional lighting that casts a single distinct shadow, clean modern aesthetic, professional and bold.

### 2. Person in Environment (photography_person_environment.png)
**Prompt:** Material Design environmental photography, a person in a modern space defined by bold solid-colored planar walls. Crisp directional sunlight casting distinct geometric shadows, clean, functional, paper-layered aesthetic.

### 3. Lifestyle Scene (photography_person_lifestyle.png)
**Prompt:** Material Design lifestyle photography, interaction with clean, tactile, paper-like objects on a desk. Photographed from above with a single strong directional light source, creating distinct uniform drop shadows, highly organized.

### 4. Ecommerce Product (photography_product_ecommerce.png)
**Prompt:** Material Design product photography, a clean geometric object resting on a bold colored background. A single strong light source creates a perfect, distinct drop shadow. Tactile, paper-like finish, highly functional and crisp.

### 5. Product Detail (photography_product_detail.png)
**Prompt:** Material Design macro photography, close-up of overlapping layers of matte, brightly colored material. Crisp lighting casting a distinct shadow from one layer onto the next, illustrating physical depth, clean geometric aesthetic.

### 6. Product in Use (photography_product_in_use.png)
**Prompt:** Material Design product-in-use photography, hands organizing physical overlapping cards or flat objects on a bold background. Directional lighting emphasizes the physical layering and z-axis depth through distinct shadows.

### 7. Place Exterior (photography_place_exterior.png)
**Prompt:** Material Design architectural exterior, a modern building composed of intersecting bold planar walls. Bright sunlight casting sharp, distinct geometric shadows, emphasizing the physical layering of the architecture, primary colors.

### 8. Place Interior (photography_place_interior.png)
**Prompt:** Material Design interior photography, a highly functional, clean modern room with bold color-blocking. Bright, crisp directional sunlight casting distinct shadows that define the physical layers of the space, orderly and tactile.

### 9. Place Detail (photography_place_detail.png)
**Prompt:** Material Design architectural detail, close crop of a clean planar wall casting a sharp distinct shadow over another brightly colored wall. Emphasizing layering, depth, and physical lighting rules, completely matte surfaces.

### 10. Abstract Atmosphere (photography_abstract.png)
**Prompt:** Material Design abstract photography, an arrangement of layered colored paper or matte cards. A single crisp light source casts distinct, realistic drop shadows, demonstrating z-axis depth and the tactile reality of physical layers.
