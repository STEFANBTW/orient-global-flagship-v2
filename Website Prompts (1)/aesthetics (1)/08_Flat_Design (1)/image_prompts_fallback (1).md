# Image Generation Prompts for Flat Design

If you run out of image generation quota, use these 10 highly-tailored Midjourney/DALL-E prompts to generate the photography assets for this aesthetic.

---

### 1. Person Portrait (photography_person_portrait.png)
**Prompt:** Flat Design photography, portrait of a person against a bright solid-color background, even flat lighting, bright vibrant clothing, minimal shadows, clean graphic composition, 2D illustration feel.

### 2. Person in Environment (photography_person_environment.png)
**Prompt:** Flat Design environmental photography, a person in an ultra-minimalist space with bright solid-color walls. Even studio lighting, completely flattened perspective, highly graphic and simple composition, lacking depth.

### 3. Lifestyle Scene (photography_person_lifestyle.png)
**Prompt:** Flat Design lifestyle photography, a person interacting with simple brightly colored objects. The scene is shot from directly above or straight on to eliminate perspective, extremely flat even lighting, graphic art style.

### 4. Ecommerce Product (photography_product_ecommerce.png)
**Prompt:** Flat Design product photography, a simple object with a matte finish resting on a bright solid-color background. Shot perfectly straight-on, completely flat even lighting, minimal shadows, graphic and bold.

### 5. Product Detail (photography_product_detail.png)
**Prompt:** Flat Design macro photography, an extreme close-up of a matte surface featuring bold solid colors intersecting. Flat lighting, zero depth of field, purely graphic composition resembling vector art.

### 6. Product in Use (photography_product_in_use.png)
**Prompt:** Flat Design product-in-use photography, hands holding a simple, brightly colored object. Shot from straight above (knolling style), bright even light, no cast shadows, feeling like a 2D digital illustration.

### 7. Place Exterior (photography_place_exterior.png)
**Prompt:** Flat Design architectural exterior, a building with completely flat facades and bright solid colors, shot straight-on to remove perspective, flat midday lighting, clear blue sky, highly graphic and illustrative.

### 8. Place Interior (photography_place_interior.png)
**Prompt:** Flat Design interior photography, a room stripped of all textures, featuring bold color-blocked walls and simple geometric furniture. Even, flat lighting that removes depth, highly stylized 2D graphic aesthetic.

### 9. Place Detail (photography_place_detail.png)
**Prompt:** Flat Design architectural detail, close crop of two intersecting walls painted in bold, contrasting solid colors. Shot perfectly flat to the camera, eliminating perspective, vector art aesthetics, zero texture.

### 10. Abstract Atmosphere (photography_abstract.png)
**Prompt:** Flat Design abstract photography, a purely graphic composition of overlapping flat paper cutouts in bright vibrant colors. Completely flat lighting, no gradients, minimal shadows, vector-like simplicity.
