# Image Generation Prompts for Art Deco

If you run out of image generation quota, use these 10 highly-tailored Midjourney/DALL-E prompts to generate the photography assets for this aesthetic.

---

### 1. Person Portrait (photography_person_portrait.png)
**Prompt:** Art Deco portrait photography, subject styled with 1920s high-glamour, sharp geometric jewelry, dramatic studio lighting, high contrast, luxurious, symmetrical framing, decadent and glamorous aesthetic.

### 2. Person in Environment (photography_person_environment.png)
**Prompt:** Art Deco environmental portrait, a person in a lavish, grand space featuring sharp geometric patterns, gold leaf, and black marble. Dramatic, glamorous lighting, perfectly symmetrical composition, opulent 1920s style.

### 3. Lifestyle Scene (photography_person_lifestyle.png)
**Prompt:** Art Deco lifestyle photography, elegant interaction with luxurious, geometrically designed objects. A lavish, glamorous setting featuring black and gold, high contrast lighting, roaring twenties opulence.

### 4. Ecommerce Product (photography_product_ecommerce.png)
**Prompt:** Art Deco product photography, a luxurious object featuring sharp, symmetrical geometric sunburst patterns in gold and black. Dramatic studio lighting, high contrast, reflecting immense glamour and wealth.

### 5. Product Detail (photography_product_detail.png)
**Prompt:** Art Deco macro photography, extreme close-up of a luxurious surface—intricate gold geometric line-work inlaid in black marble. Dramatic lighting catching the metallic shine, highly symmetrical and opulent.

### 6. Product in Use (photography_product_in_use.png)
**Prompt:** Art Deco product-in-use photography, elegantly gloved or jeweled hands interacting with a lavish, geometrically shaped object. Dramatic lighting, gold and black color palette, immense glamour and luxury.

### 7. Place Exterior (photography_place_exterior.png)
**Prompt:** Art Deco architectural exterior, a towering skyscraper from the 1920s featuring sharp, symmetrical setbacks and ornate geometric metalwork. Dramatic twilight lighting, lavish, powerful, opulent architecture.

### 8. Place Interior (photography_place_interior.png)
**Prompt:** Art Deco interior photography, a grand, highly symmetrical lobby featuring black marble floors, towering gold geometric pillars, and lavish sunburst motifs. Dramatic, opulent lighting, roaring twenties glamour.

### 9. Place Detail (photography_place_detail.png)
**Prompt:** Art Deco architectural detail, close crop of an ornate elevator door or building facade featuring sharp, highly symmetrical brass geometric patterns against dark stone. Dramatic lighting, lavish texture.

### 10. Abstract Atmosphere (photography_abstract.png)
**Prompt:** Art Deco abstract photography, a pure study of sharp, symmetrical geometric forms in gleaming gold and deep black. Dramatic high-contrast lighting, capturing the essence of 1920s lavish opulence.
