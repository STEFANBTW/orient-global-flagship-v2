# Image Generation Prompts for Luxury / High-End

If you run out of image generation quota, use these 10 highly-tailored Midjourney/DALL-E prompts to generate the photography assets for this aesthetic.

---

### 1. Person Portrait (photography_person_portrait.png)
**Prompt:** Luxury high-end portrait photography, subject exuding understated elegance, dark cinematic lighting, deep blacks, subtle highlights on skin and jewelry, sophisticated, expensive, moody editorial style.

### 2. Person in Environment (photography_person_environment.png)
**Prompt:** Luxury high-end environmental portrait, a person in an incredibly sophisticated, dark, moody interior space featuring marble and velvet. Deep shadows, highly controlled cinematic lighting, understated opulence.

### 3. Lifestyle Scene (photography_person_lifestyle.png)
**Prompt:** Luxury high-end lifestyle photography, a highly curated and elegant interaction. Dark, moody cinematic lighting highlighting premium materials like gold or crystal against deep charcoal backgrounds, quiet luxury.

### 4. Ecommerce Product (photography_product_ecommerce.png)
**Prompt:** Luxury high-end product photography, an exquisite expensive object resting on black marble. Dark, moody studio lighting with precise, sharp specular highlights catching the edges, pure black background, incredibly premium.

### 5. Product Detail (photography_product_detail.png)
**Prompt:** Luxury high-end macro photography, extreme close-up of a premium texture—brushed gold, rich velvet, or polished marble. Deep shadows, sharp focused highlights, communicating immense value and craftsmanship.

### 6. Product in Use (photography_product_in_use.png)
**Prompt:** Luxury high-end product-in-use photography, elegant, perfectly manicured hands interacting with a premium object. Dark cinematic lighting, deep black negative space, understated opulence and sophistication.

### 7. Place Exterior (photography_place_exterior.png)
**Prompt:** Luxury high-end architectural exterior, a stunning modern architectural masterpiece at twilight. Moody atmospheric lighting, deep shadows, warm glowing interior lights, exuding exclusivity and immense wealth.

### 8. Place Interior (photography_place_interior.png)
**Prompt:** Luxury high-end interior photography, an incredibly sophisticated and dark room, featuring rich dark woods, black marble, and subtle gold accents. Directional cinematic lighting, deep shadows, quiet luxury.

### 9. Place Detail (photography_place_detail.png)
**Prompt:** Luxury high-end architectural detail, close crop of polished brass hardware against dark, heavily grained wood or black marble. Sharp, controlled lighting creating elegant specular highlights, premium aesthetic.

### 10. Abstract Atmosphere (photography_abstract.png)
**Prompt:** Luxury high-end abstract photography, a moody, dark study of light reflecting off a polished gold or crystal surface against a pure black background. Cinematic, sophisticated, deeply elegant.
