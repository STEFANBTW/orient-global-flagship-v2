# Image Generation Prompts for Vaporwave Y2K

If you run out of image generation quota, use these 10 highly-tailored Midjourney/DALL-E prompts to generate the photography assets for this aesthetic.

### 1. Person Portrait (photography_person_portrait.png)
**Prompt:** Vaporwave Y2K portrait photography, subject wearing retro-futuristic glossy clothing, soft surreal lighting in pastel pinks and purples, early 2000s cyber aesthetic, dreamlike, subtle chromatic aberration.

### 2. Person in Environment (photography_person_environment.png)
**Prompt:** Vaporwave Y2K environmental photography, a person standing in a surreal, dreamlike space featuring classical statues and early computer graphics imagery. Bathed in soft pastel pink and magenta lighting, nostalgic and liminal.

### 3. Lifestyle Scene (photography_person_lifestyle.png)
**Prompt:** Vaporwave Y2K lifestyle photography, interaction with translucent glossy plastic objects or early 2000s tech (like a clear Gameboy). Soft surreal pastel lighting, nostalgic cyber aesthetic, dreamy and highly stylized.

### 4. Ecommerce Product (photography_product_ecommerce.png)
**Prompt:** Vaporwave Y2K product photography, a retro-futuristic glossy object resting on a reflective grid floor. Soft gradient lighting in cyan and magenta, surreal studio environment, early digital aesthetic.

### 5. Product Detail (photography_product_detail.png)
**Prompt:** Vaporwave Y2K macro photography, extreme close-up of translucent colored plastic or iridescent holographic material. Soft pastel light refracting through it, dreamy, nostalgic early 2000s texture, soft focus.

### 6. Product in Use (photography_product_in_use.png)
**Prompt:** Vaporwave Y2K product-in-use photography, hands with metallic nail polish interacting with a retro digital interface or glossy tech device. Surreal pastel lighting, nostalgic cyber-utopian aesthetic.

### 7. Place Exterior (photography_place_exterior.png)
**Prompt:** Vaporwave Y2K architectural exterior, a surreal, dead-mall exterior or postmodern 80s building set against a gradient pink and purple sky. Dreamlike, liminal space aesthetic, nostalgic and strangely empty.

### 8. Place Interior (photography_place_interior.png)
**Prompt:** Vaporwave Y2K interior photography, a liminal interior space featuring glass blocks, indoor palm trees, and checkerboard tiles. Soft surreal pastel lighting, nostalgic late 80s/early 90s aesthetic, dreamy and abandoned.

### 9. Place Detail (photography_place_detail.png)
**Prompt:** Vaporwave Y2K architectural detail, close crop of glass block walls reflecting cyan and magenta neon light, juxtaposed with a plastic palm frond. Surreal, highly nostalgic, glossy textures.

### 10. Abstract Atmosphere (photography_abstract.png)
**Prompt:** Vaporwave Y2K abstract photography, a surreal blend of a wireframe grid and a classical marble texture, bathed in soft pastel pink and blue gradients. Dreamy, nostalgic early internet aesthetic, chromatic aberration.
