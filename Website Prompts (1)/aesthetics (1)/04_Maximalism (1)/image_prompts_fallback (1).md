# Image Generation Prompts for Maximalism

If you run out of image generation quota, use these 10 highly-tailored Midjourney/DALL-E prompts to generate the photography assets for this aesthetic.

---

### 1. Person Portrait (photography_person_portrait.png)
**Prompt:** Maximalist portrait photography, close-up, eclectic styling, bold clashing patterns, vibrant highly saturated colors, visually dense background, unapologetically loud, avant-garde fashion.

### 2. Person in Environment (photography_person_environment.png)
**Prompt:** Maximalist environmental portrait, full figure in an incredibly opulent and visually dense room, layered textures, rich saturated jewel tones, over-the-top decor, baroque meets modern chaos.

### 3. Lifestyle Scene (photography_person_lifestyle.png)
**Prompt:** Maximalist lifestyle photography, a chaotic but highly curated scene, mixing vintage and modern objects, clashing neon and jewel tones, intense visual richness, luxurious clutter.

### 4. Ecommerce Product (photography_product_ecommerce.png)
**Prompt:** Maximalist product photography, a bold vibrant object set against a wildly patterned background, dramatic surreal lighting, contrasting colors, visually overwhelming but intentional.

### 5. Product Detail (photography_product_detail.png)
**Prompt:** Maximalist macro photography, extreme close-up of an intensely detailed surface, sequins, velvet, and gold colliding, highly saturated colors, overwhelming texture, rich visual depth.

### 6. Product in Use (photography_product_in_use.png)
**Prompt:** Maximalist product-in-use photography, heavily jeweled or patterned hands holding a vibrant object, surrounded by colorful contrasting props, intense visual energy, opulent styling.

### 7. Place Exterior (photography_place_exterior.png)
**Prompt:** Maximalist architectural exterior photography, an eccentric building covered in vibrant murals or lush overgrown foliage, eclectic design, loud colors, surreal and striking composition.

### 8. Place Interior (photography_place_interior.png)
**Prompt:** Maximalist interior photography, a room bursting with clashing wallpaper, velvet furniture, gallery walls filled floor-to-ceiling with art, intense color saturation, curated chaos.

### 9. Place Detail (photography_place_detail.png)
**Prompt:** Maximalist architectural detail photography, close crop of clashing ornate materials, gold leaf meeting neon acrylic, visually dense, highly saturated, eclectic and rebellious.

### 10. Abstract Atmosphere (photography_abstract.png)
**Prompt:** Maximalist abstract photography, a wild explosion of colorful liquids or fabrics mixing, chaotic energy, highly saturated neons and jewel tones, overwhelming visual noise, dynamic composition.
