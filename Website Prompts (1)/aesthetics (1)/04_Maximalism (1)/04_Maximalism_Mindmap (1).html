    • 🏛️ Ranked Industries
      • 1. Entertainment & Music — matches the energy, visual spectacle and excitement
      • 2. Luxury Fashion (Haute Couture) — opulent, statement-heavy brand alignment (e.g. Versace)
      • 3. Nightlife / Event Promotion — communicates high-stimulation nightlife culture
      • 4. Beauty & Cosmetics (Bold/Expressive) — color-rich branding illustrating product potential
      • 5. Gaming (AAA Titles) — mirrors immersive, sensory-rich gaming worlds
      • 6. Theme Parks & Entertainment Venues — maps promises of immersive adventures
      • 7. Food & Beverage (Indulgent Brands) — triggers instant mouthwatering desire/indulgence
      • 8. Interior Design (Eclectic/Baroque) — mirrors layered textiles and textures
      • 9. Streetwear & Sneaker Culture — matches the culture of hype and visual excess
      • 10. Wedding & Event Planning — translates the more-is-more philosophy of celebration

    • 📐 Spacing & Layout
      • Layered Density
        • Section Padding: 80px - 100px (Shorter scroll rhythm)
        • Overlap elements to create dimensional depth
      • Grid Distortions
        • Asymmetric overlapping columns
        • Rotate cards and elements 3 to 8 degrees
        • Bleed visual sections across adjacent block boundaries
      • Launch Spacing & Borders Studio
    • ⬡ Borders & Shapes
      • Borders & Outlines
        • Double borders (border + outline with offset gap)
        • Thick accent borders (4px to 8px) on featured blocks
      • Mixed Border Radius
        • Varying radii (fully rounded 24px-32px cards vs 0px sharp images)
      • Shapes & Patterns
        • Velvet, marble, gold foil repeating backdrop patterns
        • Irregular fluid blob forms as color backdrops
        • Hard solid drop shadows (multi-colored, offset, no blur)
    • ▢ UI Components
      • Navigation
        • Overlapping header cards at viewport top
        • Hover outline expansion and instant color flashing
      • Buttons
        • Gold/pink fill, double border outlines, 3D spring-bounce scaling
        • Outlined secondary buttons with thick clashing outlines
      • Cards
        • Layered cards overlapping neighboring visual spaces
        • Marble textures, thick gold borders, hard shadow outlines
      • Launch UI Components Sandbox
    • 🖼️ Imagery & Texture
      • Photography Rules
        • Professional dramatic studio color grading and composite layering
        • Highly styled models, props, and elaborate environments
      • Textures & Patterns
        • Velvet fabric sheens, swirling marble, damask repeating details
        • Heavy photography grain overlay (15% to 25% opacity)
        • Multi-stop gradient meshes shifting slowly in the background
      • Launch Imagery & Texture Lab
    • ⚡ Animation & Motion
      • Allowed Motion
        • Entrance slides that overshoot and snap (Spring physics)
        • Multi-speed layer parallax coordinates scrolling
        • Hue-shifting gradient color background loops
        • Text mask wipe reveals and custom cursor trails
      • Animation Avoids
        • No loading times over 3s (Optimize large mesh files)
        • No seizure-inducing speed loops or constant flashing
      • Launch Animation Studio
    • ⚠️ Strict Avoids
      • No Flat white background bases (Must feel rich/saturated)
      • No Unrelated random color palettes (Must maintain analogous/jewel logic)
      • No Poorly optimized images causing slow loading (>3s)
      • No Inconsistent production value across section cards
      • No Plain, unadorned empty boxes or sections
