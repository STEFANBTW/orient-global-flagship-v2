02 Brutalism — Interactive Mindmap

  • 02 — Brutalism −
    • Ranked Industries −
      • 1. Music / Record Labels (Indie) −
        • Anti-establishment energy of independent music culture translates naturally.
      • 2. Avant-Garde Fashion / Streetwear −
        • Brands that reject convention use brutalism to signal they do not want conventional customers.
      • 3. Art Galleries & Contemporary Art −
        • Brutalism mirrors the confrontational nature of contemporary conceptual art.
      • 4. Publishing / Literary Magazines −
        • Print-culture rawness translates naturally to brutalist digital design.
      • 5. Architecture Firms (Experimental) −
        • Direct physical reference; experimental architects use it as a signal of theoretical seriousness.
      • 6. Creative Agencies & Design Studios −
        • Self-promotion for designers who want to demonstrate that they think differently.
      • 7. Gaming / Esports (Indie) −
        • The anti-corporate rawness aligns with indie gaming culture.
      • 8. Food & Beverage (Provocateur Brands) −
        • Hot sauces, craft spirits that want to look like they don't care about mainstream approval.
      • 9. Tech Startups (Anti-Corporate) −
        • Startups deliberately using brutalism to signal they are building against the establishment.
      • 10. Comedy / Entertainment −
        • The absurdist, rule-breaking quality of brutalism mirrors comedic subversion.
    • Psychological Foundation −
      • Honesty Over Beauty −
        • Deliberately exposes raw systems and elements that conventional design hides.
      • Violation of Expectation −
        • Arrests the eye and forces attention by breaking mainstream patterns.
    • Color System −
      • Palette Logic −
        • Blunt, unmodulated saturation. Extremes with zero compromise.
      • Dominant Palettes −
        • Backgrounds: #FFFFFF or #000000 or #E0E0E0
        • Screaming Accent: Acid Yellow #FFFF00, Neon Lime #00FF00, Cyan #00FFFF, Magenta #FF00FF
