04 Maximalism — Interactive Mindmap

  • 04 — Maximalism −
    • Ranked Industries −
      • 1. Entertainment & Music −
        • Entertainment celebrates abundance; maximalism matches the spectacle.
      • 2. Luxury Fashion (Haute Couture) −
        • Versace, D&G, Gucci are definitionally maximalist.
      • 3. Nightlife / Event Promotion −
        • Clubs and festivals communicate excitement and excess.
      • 4. Beauty & Cosmetics (Bold) −
        • Color-rich brands demonstrate what their products enable.
      • 5. Gaming (AAA / Console) −
        • Visual richness of game worlds reflected in promotional design.
      • 6. Theme Parks & Entertainment −
        • Communicates promise of overwhelming, immersive experience.
      • 7. Food & Beverage (Indulgent) −
        • Chocolates, desserts, spirits promise sensory indulgence.
      • 8. Interior Design (Eclectic) −
        • Layered textiles, pattern, and color as brand demonstration.
      • 9. Streetwear & Sneaker Culture −
        • Visual language of hype, collaboration, and excess.
      • 10. Wedding & Event Planning −
        • More-is-more philosophy of celebration.
    • Psychological Foundation −
      • Sensory Promise −
        • Design delivers richness as preview of product. Chocolate brand's site makes you taste before reading.
      • Controlled Curation −
        • System hidden under layers of richness. Viewer experiences abundance, never confusion. Distinction: confusion = noise.
    • Color System −
      • Palette Logic −
        • Gold never appears alone — it always exists alongside other rich colors.
      • Dominant Palettes −
        • Analogous + Contrast: #4A0E8F, #E91E8C, #CC1A4A, #E85D04 + #00B4D8
        • Jewel Tones: #1C3D8C, #1A7A4A, #8B1A2E, #5C1A8C, #B8860B
        • Maximalist Warmth: #C44B1A, #D4A017, #F0D58C, #C48B9F, #2D5A27
        • Backgrounds: #1E0A30 (Burgundy)
        • Gold Universal: #C9A96E (Light Gold), #D4AF37 (Medium Gold), #B8860B (Deep Gold)
    • Typography −
      • Classification −
        • Decorative Serifs: Playfair Display. Slab Serifs: Zilla Slab. Sans: DM Sans.
