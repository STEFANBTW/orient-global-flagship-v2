01 Minimalism — Interactive Mindmap

  • 01 — Minimalism −
    • Ranked Industries −
      • 1. Luxury Watchmaking −
        • Minimalism is the language of high-end watches; restraint signals precision and exclusivity.
      • 2. Premium SaaS / Productivity −
        • Clean interfaces reduce cognitive load; perfection feels expensive.
      • 3. High-End Interior Design / Architecture −
        • Minimalism is the founding philosophy of modern interior design.
      • 4. Luxury Fashion / Haute Couture −
        • White space on a runway, on a website, signals confidence and exclusivity.
      • 5. Medical / Healthcare Tech −
        • Clarity and calmness are critical; minimalism reduces anxiety in clinical contexts.
      • 6. Fine Art Galleries / Museums −
        • The content (art) should dominate; the site should disappear.
      • 7. Skincare / Cosmetics (Premium) −
        • Ingredients and simplicity are selling points; overcomplicated visuals undermine the message.
      • 8. Legal / Consultancy Services −
        • Trust is built on clarity; communicates control without needing to oversell.
      • 9. Financial Planning / Wealth Management −
        • Clutter creates anxiety; clean design communicates stability and competence.
      • 10. Photography Studios (Independent) −
        • The photographs are the product; the website must be an invisible frame.
    • Psychological Foundation −
      • Cognitive Load Elimination −
        • Strips design down to only what is essential, eliminating visual friction. Brain processes the page effortlessly.
      • Scarcity Signals Value −
        • White space is expensive. Showing almost nothing communicates extreme brand confidence.
    • Color System −
      • Palette Logic −
        • Max of three colors (ideally two): background, foreground, and optionally one accent used ruthlessly sparingly.
      • Background Colors −
        • Barely-there grey-white: #FAFAFA (Safe/universal)
        • Warm cream white: #F7F5F2 (Fashion/artisanal)
        • Deeper warm cream: #F0EEE9 (Luxury/jewellery)
        • Pure clinical white: #FFFFFF (Tech/medical)
      • Foreground / Text −
        • Near-black: #1A1A1A (Standard body/headings)
        • Editorial near-black: #2C2C2C (Lighter body)
        • Headlines near-black: #111111 (Maximum typographic weight)
      • Accent Colors −
        • Warm: Terracotta #C4674F, Dusty Rose #B87D7D, Gold #C9A96E
        • Cool: Navy #1B3A5C, Forest Green #2D5016, Slate #4A6FA5
        • Neutral: Warm Grey #8C8480, Muted Sage #7A9E7E
    • Typography −
      • Foundational Rule −
        • Typography does not carry information — it IS the design. Choices carry the entire visual weight.

    • What to Avoid −
      • ❌ Gradients — they break flatness and add visual complexity.
      • ❌ Saturated backgrounds — they collapse the white space.
      • ❌ Drop shadows — they imply physical layers where minimalism demands absolute flatness.
      • ❌ Hover scale effects — cards should not pop or grow. Instead use simple color flips or border weight shifts.
